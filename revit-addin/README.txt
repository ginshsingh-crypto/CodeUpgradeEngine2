========================================
  LOD 400 Uploader - Revit Add-in
========================================

QUICK INSTALLATION:
-------------------
1. Build the add-in (if not already built):
   - Open Command Prompt in this folder
   - Run: dotnet restore
   - Run: dotnet build -c Release

2. Install:
   - Double-click "Install.bat"
   - The installer will automatically copy files to your Revit add-ins folder

3. Use:
   - Restart Revit
   - Look for "LOD 400" tab in the ribbon
   - Click "Upload Sheets" to get started

FIRST TIME USERS:
-----------------
When you click "Upload Sheets":
1. Click "Create Account" tab
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Create Account"
4. Switch to "Sign In" tab and login

WHAT THIS ADD-IN DOES:
----------------------
- Select sheets from your Revit model
- Upload your model for professional shop drawings
- Each sheet costs 150 SAR
- Receive completed shop drawings within 24 hours

MANUAL INSTALLATION:
--------------------
If the installer doesn't work, copy these files manually:

From: LOD400Uploader\bin\Release\net48\LOD400Uploader.dll
To:   %APPDATA%\Autodesk\Revit\Addins\2024\LOD400Uploader.dll

From: LOD400Uploader\LOD400Uploader.addin
To:   %APPDATA%\Autodesk\Revit\Addins\2024\LOD400Uploader.addin

(Change "2024" to your Revit version: 2022, 2023, or 2024)
